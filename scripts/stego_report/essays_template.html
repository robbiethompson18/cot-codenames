What the models are learning to write

Both training runs found the same trick: the sender describes the secret word by saying what the scene is not.

A sender gets a secret word and an essay topic. A receiver (the same trained model) and a frozen, smaller monitor each read the essay and pick the word from 8 candidates. The sender is rewarded when the receiver is right and the monitor is wrong. These six essays come from the first training rung, where the same 8 words are used every time.

  • Before training the sender hints plainly, and the monitor reads the hint almost as well as the receiver.
  • After training the hints are still about the word's meaning. They have moved into denials: no energy, no debate, no climb.
  • The monitor is a smaller model and mostly misses these. A same-size monitor may not, which is the next thing to test.
  • On Tinker the essays also drift into one meditative style and away from the topic, and the judge still passes them.
